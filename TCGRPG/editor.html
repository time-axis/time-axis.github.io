Pack makeup and rarity chances

Remaining slots are flexible (Common or Above). Set weights below for each rarity; the default weights total 100%. Rare cards have an 8% chance in a flexible slot. Each higher rarity tier is one-quarter as likely as the previous tier, with RH/RS and RP/RH-LG splitting their respective tiers. For Uncommon+ slots, Common odds shift to Uncommon, keeping the 8% Rare chance. For guaranteed Rare+ slots, only Rare and higher weights compete.
